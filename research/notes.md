## Useful source claims (summarized)

The idea proposes internal-affiliate-style cross-promotion for independent apps: a “you might also like” placement that credits developers for new installs. The stated pressure is a rapid increase in subscription-app creation, projected growth in Product Hunt submissions, and rising paid acquisition costs. The suggested playbook is inspired by long-running mobile-game cross-promotion.

Illustrative figures supplied by the source: new subscription apps rose from roughly 2,000 to nearly 15,000 per month in under three years; Product Hunt could see 5× more submissions this year; a cited average cost per acquired mobile user is $6. These are directional newsletter claims, not independently verified market estimates.
